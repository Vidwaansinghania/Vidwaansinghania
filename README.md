# Vidwaan Singhania

I'm a BCom student at UVic Gustavson in Victoria, BC, graduating in 2027. I build AI agents and automation for finance work.

| Project | What it is |
|---|---|
| [Multi-Agent-Equity-Research](https://github.com/Vidwaansinghania/Multi-Agent-Equity-Research) | Ten agents in eight stages research a company, argue both sides and make a BUY, HOLD or SELL call. Each call gets scored against the market a year later. |
| [Multi Agent Whitepaper](https://vidwaansinghania.github.io/Multi-Agent-Equity-Research/) | A 10-page white paper, readable online. I wrote equity research on eighteen companies, ran the ten agents on six of them and compared the calls. The two methods gave the same rating on five of the six. On Alphabet the agents said SELL where I said HOLD. |
| [I-to-E-Framework](https://github.com/Vidwaansinghania/I-to-E-Framework) | A five-stage operating model framework: diagnose, design, stress-test, build, run and evolve. I built it by putting four published frameworks from McKinsey, Bain, Accenture and KPMG in order, and tested it by reading Unilever's 2024 reorganization against the stages. A two-page [explainer](https://vidwaansinghania.github.io/I-to-E-Framework/) is online. |
| [The-House-Always-Wins](https://github.com/Vidwaansinghania/The-House-Always-Wins) | A tracker for stock trades disclosed by members of the US Congress. It reads new filings from the House Clerk and Senate eFD sites, has agents check and analyse them, and rebuilds an infographic. |
| [Finance-Skills](https://github.com/Vidwaansinghania/Finance-Skills) | Four open-source AI skills that form an investment committee: an analyst, a risk officer, a macro analyst and a portfolio manager. They work with any model that can load a prompt. |
| [Reconciliation-Assistant](https://github.com/Vidwaansinghania/Reconciliation-Assistant) | A variance commentary checker. An AI skill drafts month-end budget-vs-actual commentary, and a script checks every number, direction word and missing driver against the source before a reviewer signs off. |
| [Self-Improver-Loop](https://github.com/Vidwaansinghania/Self-Improver-Loop) | An AI skill that reads your recent coding-assistant sessions, reports what keeps going wrong, and checks on the next review whether the fixes worked. |
| [I-Hate-AI-Writing](https://github.com/Vidwaansinghania/I-Hate-AI-Writing) | An AI skill with the rules that keep generated prose from reading like a language model wrote it: banned vocabulary, plain swaps and a final pass to run before delivering text. |
